Pricing questions

What exactly is free on the START plan?

Crypto market data, the standard chart types and the classic indicator set, on one connection, with no time limit. Cluster charts, profiles and the order-flow scanners require a paid tier.

How do I start the trial?

Activate a 14-day trial of any paid plan from inside the platform. Everything is unlocked for the duration. Buy a plan up front and you get an additional 14 days on top of the subscription.

Is annual billing really 30% cheaper?

Yes — the yearly figures shown above are the effective monthly cost when you pay for twelve months at once.

What does a lifetime licence include?

A one-off payment for permanent access to that tier with no recurring subscription. Market data fees from your provider are separate either way.

Can I change plans later?

You can upgrade or downgrade at any renewal. Upgrading mid-cycle is prorated against what you have already paid.

Are exchange fees included?

No. Futures and equities data is billed by your feed provider or exchange. Crypto exchange data comes over public APIs and is included.
