Cluster charts

Footprints that show intent

Each bar is decomposed into price levels, and each level into the volume that hit the bid versus the volume that lifted the ask. Where one side keeps paying up you get an imbalance; where they pay up and price stops moving, you get absorption.

  • Cluster modes: Bid × Ask, Delta, Volume, Trades, Delta %
  • Configurable imbalance ratio and stacked-imbalance depth
  • Per-bar POC, value area and delta footer
  • Tick, volume, range, delta, Renko and time-based bars

Market Profile

Structure, not just levels

Proper Market Profile and TPO charting: value area, point of control, single prints and initial balance, built per session and per instrument. Volume profiles can be anchored to any range you draw.

  • TPO letters, split profiles and composite sessions
  • Dynamic POC, developing value area and naked POCs
  • Custom session boundaries — essential in 24/7 crypto
  • Anchored VWAP with standard-deviation bands

Smart DOM

Trade from the ladder

The DOM is where order flow becomes a decision. Smart DOM keeps resting liquidity, executed volume, your working orders and your position in one narrow column that updates at feed speed.

  • One-click entries, OCO brackets, break-even and trailing
  • Liquidity change tracking to catch pulled and iceberg orders
  • Session volume histogram docked to the ladder
  • Full hotkey control and per-instrument risk limits

Smart Tape & scanners

Find the trades that matter

Raw time & sales is unreadable at speed. These tools group, filter and flag it for you.

Smart Tape

Consolidates raw prints into real trades and shows aggression strength, speed of tape, imbalance and open interest as it goes.

Big Trades

Flags unusually large single or aggregated trades — the ones going against the general flow are usually the interesting ones.

Cluster Search

Define the cluster conditions you trade and scan both history and live data for them. Where Big Trades finds single prints, Cluster Search finds total volume inside a cluster.
