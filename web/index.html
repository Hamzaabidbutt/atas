    The platform

    Everything the tape is telling you

    Price shows you what happened. Order flow shows you how it happened — who paid up, who got absorbed, and where the book thinned out.

    Cluster (footprint) charts

    Every candle broken down into bid × ask volume at each price. Spot absorption, exhaustion and stacked imbalances at a glance.

    Market Profile & TPO

    Full session profiles with value area, POC and TPO structure — see how long price actually spent at every level, not just that it traded there.

    Smart DOM

    A depth-of-market ladder built for speed: resting liquidity, pulled orders, executed volume and one-click order handling in a single column.

    Delta & CVD

    Delta, cumulative delta and CVD Pro track aggression bar by bar and session by session, so divergences show up before price confirms them.

    Cluster Search & Big Trades

    Scan history and live data for the exact conditions you trade — volume spikes, imbalance stacks, or single prints above your size threshold.

    Smart Tape

    Raw prints grouped into real trades, with aggression strength, speed of tape and open interest — the time & sales you can actually read.

    Cluster analysis

    Inside every candle

    A cluster chart splits each bar into price levels and shows the volume that traded on the bid versus the ask. Imbalances stack where one side keeps paying up; absorption shows where they stopped being rewarded.

    • Bid × Ask, delta, volume and trade-count cluster modes
    • Automatic POC, value area and stacked-imbalance marking
    • Tick, volume, range, delta and Renko bar building
    • Heatmap mode for liquidity that never prints on the chart

    FAQ

    Questions before you download

    Is there really a free plan?

    Yes. Installing ATAS puts you on the START plan, which is free for as long as you want it. It covers crypto data and a basic set of charts and indicators. Cluster charts and the advanced order-flow tools live on the paid tiers.

    How does the trial work?

    You can activate a 14-day trial of any paid plan at any point, with full functionality unlocked. Buy a plan straight away and you get an extra 14 days on top.

    Do I need a separate data subscription?

    For futures and equities, yes — ATAS connects to your own feed (Rithmic, CQG, Interactive Brokers and others) and exchange fees are billed by the provider. Crypto exchange data is included.

    Which operating systems are supported?

    ATAS is a native Windows application (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit). Traders on macOS and Linux typically run it on a Windows VPS or a virtual machine.

    Can I write my own indicators?

    Yes. The Pro and Ultra tiers expose the strategy and indicator API so you can build custom studies and automate execution.

    What happens to my lifetime licence?

    A lifetime licence is a one-off payment for permanent access to that tier, including updates within it — there is no recurring subscription afterwards.
